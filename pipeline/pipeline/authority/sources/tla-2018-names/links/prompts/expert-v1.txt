{rules}

How Dodson & Hilton write other names. Each line is another name as Dodson & Hilton print it, with the TLA transliteration an earlier step of this work decided is the same name, or "no TLA name" where it decided that none of that name's candidates is. These are earlier decisions, not ground truth. They show how Dodson & Hilton write names; they do not say which candidate, if any, is record 1's name, and the rules above alone decide whether two spellings are one name.
{conventions}
